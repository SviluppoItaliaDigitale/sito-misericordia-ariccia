  - id: "ictus"
    titolo: "Stroke"
    motto: "Time is brain"
    riconosci: "Suddenly one side of the face droops, an arm loses strength, the person slurs their speech or cannot understand. **A single sign** is enough."
    fare:
      - "**Face:** ask them to smile. Does one side of the mouth stay down?"
      - "**Arms:** ask them to raise both arms. Does one drift down or not move?"
      - "**Speech:** ask them to repeat a simple sentence. Is their speech slurred or muddled, or do they not understand?"
      - "Even one sign: **call 112 immediately** and **note the time** the symptoms started (or the last time the person was well)."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't wait for it to pass, even if the symptoms seem to improve."
      - "Don't give food, drink or medicines."
  - id: "infarto"
    titolo: "Heart attack"
    motto: "Time is life"
    riconosci: "Pain, heaviness or tightness **in the centre of the chest**, which may spread to the arm (often the left), jaw, back or stomach. Often with cold sweats, nausea or shortness of breath. In women, older people and people with diabetes the signs may be less obvious."
    fare:
      - "If the pain **does not go away after a few minutes**, call **112** immediately."
      - "Help the person sit or lie down in whatever position is most comfortable for them, and reassure them."
      - "Stay with them until help arrives and follow the operator's instructions."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't drive them to hospital yourself and don't let them drive: the ambulance starts treatment during the journey."
      - "Don't underestimate an «odd» pain just because it isn't very severe."
  - id: "emorragia"
    titolo: "Severe bleeding"
    motto: "Press and don't let go"
    riconosci: "Blood flowing heavily, spurting or not stopping from a wound."
    fare:
      - "If you have gloves, put them on."
      - "Press hard **directly on the wound** with a sterile gauze or a clean cloth, and **keep up the pressure without letting go**."
      - "Call **112**."
      - "On an arm or leg, if pressure is not enough, someone who is trained can apply a **tourniquet** as soon as possible, noting the time."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't release the pressure to «check» whether the bleeding has stopped."
      - "Don't remove objects embedded in the wound: press around them."
  - id: "rianimazione"
    titolo: "Cardiac arrest"
    motto: "You can make the difference"
    riconosci: "The person **does not respond** when you call them and gently shake them, and **is not breathing normally** (not breathing at all, or taking infrequent, noisy breaths)."
    fare:
      - "Call **112** and put the phone on **speaker**: the operator will guide you."
      - "Have someone bring a **defibrillator (AED)** straight away: switch it on and follow its voice prompts."
      - "One hand on top of the other **in the centre of the chest**, arms straight: compress **5–6 cm**, **100–120 times a minute**, with as few interruptions as possible."
      - "Keep going until help arrives or the person starts breathing normally again."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't be afraid of getting it wrong: without compressions there is no chance of survival."
      - "Don't stop to check for a pulse."
  - id: "soffocamento"
    titolo: "Choking"
    motto: "5 blows, 5 thrusts"
    riconosci: "While eating, the person brings their hands to their throat, **cannot speak or cough**, is struggling to breathe or is not breathing."
    fare:
      - "If they are coughing, **encourage them to keep coughing**: it is the best way to clear the obstruction."
      - "If they cannot cough: lean them forwards and give **up to 5** firm **back blows** with the heel of your hand **between the shoulder blades**."
      - "If that doesn't work: **up to 5 abdominal thrusts** (Heimlich manoeuvre): fist above the navel, pull sharply inwards and upwards."
      - "**Alternate** 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts until the object comes out."
      - "If they become unresponsive: call **112** and start **CPR**."
      - "After abdominal thrusts the person must always be seen by a doctor."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't put your fingers blindly into their mouth."
      - "In infants and young children the technique is different: it is taught in paediatric choking and life-support courses (P-BLSD)."
  - id: "anafilassi"
    titolo: "Anaphylaxis"
    motto: "Adrenaline and 112"
    riconosci: "After a food, a medicine or an insect sting: swelling of the lips, tongue or face, difficulty breathing, widespread hives, sudden feeling of being unwell."
    fare:
      - "Call **112**."
      - "If the person has an **adrenaline auto-injector**, help them use it in the outer thigh as soon as possible."
      - "If they have not improved after **5 minutes** and have a second dose, it can be repeated."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't let them stand up suddenly: if they feel faint, have them lie down."
      - "Don't wait for the symptoms to get worse."
  - id: "ustioni"
    titolo: "Burns"
    motto: "20 minutes of water"
    riconosci: "Reddened skin, with blisters or wounds after contact with heat, boiling liquids, flames or steam."
    fare:
      - "Cool immediately under **cool running water** (not ice-cold) for **at least 20 minutes**."
      - "Remove rings, bracelets and watches before the area swells."
      - "Cover with a clean dressing or with cling film, without wrapping it tightly."
      - "Large burns, or burns to the face, hands or genitals, or in children: call **112**."
    nonfare:
      - "No ice, toothpaste, butter, oil or other home remedies."
      - "Don't pull off clothing stuck to the skin and don't burst blisters."
  - id: "ipoglicemia"
    titolo: "Hypoglycaemia"
    motto: "Sugar is needed"
    riconosci: "In a person with diabetes: sweating, shaking, pallor, confusion, strange behaviour, sudden reduced responsiveness."
    fare:
      - "If they are **awake and able to swallow**: 15–20 g of fast-acting sugar (glucose tablets, sugary sweets, 50–100 ml of fruit juice or a sugary drink)."
      - "If the symptoms have not gone after **15 minutes**, **repeat**."
      - "Once they feel better, give them a light snack."
      - "If they do not respond or do not improve: call **112**."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't give anything by mouth to someone who is not responding: they could choke."
  - id: "avvelenamento"
    titolo: "Poisoning"
    motto: "No DIY remedies"
    riconosci: "Swallowing or contact with medicines, detergents, chemicals, plants or mushrooms; breathing in gas or fumes."
    fare:
      - "Call **112** or a **Poison Control Centre** (Centro Antiveleni) and follow their instructions."
      - "Keep the product's **packaging** (or a photo of the label) to hand."
      - "If it is gas or fumes, move the person into the fresh air **without putting yourself in danger**."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't make them vomit."
      - "Don't give milk, oil or anything else to drink unless healthcare professionals tell you to."
  - id: "trauma-cranico"
    titolo: "Head injury"
    motto: "Mind the head"
    riconosci: "After a blow to the head: headache, dizziness, nausea, blurred or disturbed vision, confusion, difficulty remembering, drowsiness."
    fare:
      - "Have the person **stop immediately**: no sport or exertion."
      - "Call **112** if they lose consciousness, vomit repeatedly, are confused, very drowsy or have seizures."
      - "Even if they seem fine, have them seen by a doctor and don't leave them alone in the following hours."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't let them go back to playing or training on the same day."
      - "If there has been a fall from a height or a heavy impact, don't move their neck."
  - id: "convulsioni"
    titolo: "Seizures"
    motto: "Protect, don't restrain"
    dal: "2026-11-10"
    riconosci: "Loss of consciousness with **rhythmic jerking** of the arms and legs, sometimes frothing at the mouth or a bitten tongue. It usually stops on its own within a few minutes."
    fare:
      - "Move dangerous objects away and **clear the space around** the person."
      - "Put something **soft under their head** and loosen their collar."
      - "**Check the time**: if it lasts more than 5 minutes, call **112**."
      - "Also call 112 if it is their first seizure, if they are injured, if they are pregnant, if the seizures recur or if they do not come round."
      - "Once the movements stop, **check their breathing**: if they are breathing normally, put them **on their side** and stay with them. If they are not breathing normally, start CPR."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't restrain their movements."
      - "Don't put anything in their mouth: no fingers, no objects, no water."
  - id: "incidente-stradale"
    titolo: "Road accident"
    motto: "Safety first"
    dal: "2026-11-13"
    riconosci: "Vehicles involved, people injured or trapped, traffic still passing: the first danger is to whoever stops to help."
    fare:
      - "Stop in a safe place, switch on your **hazard lights** and put on your **high-visibility vest**."
      - "Place the **warning triangle** at least 50 metres away."
      - "If you can do so without risk, switch off the engines. No cigarettes."
      - "Call **112**: where you are, how many are injured, whether anyone is trapped."
      - "Talk to the injured, reassure them and keep them covered. If someone is not breathing normally, start CPR."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't move the injured, unless there is an immediate danger such as a fire."
      - "Don't remove a motorcyclist's helmet, unless it is needed to let them breathe."
  - id: "svenimento"
    titolo: "Fainting"
    motto: "Down, and legs up"
    dal: "2026-11-17"
    riconosci: "Beforehand: pallor, sweating, nausea, blurred vision, ringing in the ears. Then a brief loss of consciousness, usually less than a minute."
    fare:
      - "If you feel you are about to faint: **cross your legs and tense your muscles**, or squat or lie down."
      - "If they faint: **lying down with legs raised**, clothing loosened, fresh air."
      - "If they don't come round within a minute, **check their breathing** and call **112**."
      - "Once they feel better, help them up slowly: first sitting, then standing."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't let them get up suddenly."
      - "Don't underestimate fainting during exertion, with chest pain or palpitations: call 112."
  - id: "soffocamento-lattante"
    titolo: "Choking in infants"
    motto: "5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts"
    dal: "2026-11-20"
    riconosci: "A baby under one year old who **cannot cry, cough or breathe** and turns blue."
    fare:
      - "If they are coughing or crying, let them cough and keep a close eye on them."
      - "Have someone call **112**, or call on speakerphone."
      - "**5 back blows** between the shoulder blades: face down along your forearm, head lower than the body, supporting the chin."
      - "**5 chest thrusts**: face up, head down, with **two thumbs** in the centre of the chest."
      - "Alternate 5 and 5. If they become unresponsive: **5 rescue breaths** into the mouth and nose, then CPR."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't put your fingers blindly into their mouth."
      - "Don't give round foods whole: cut grapes, cherry tomatoes and olives into quarters, lengthways."
  - id: "rianimazione-bambino"
    titolo: "CPR in children"
    motto: "First, 5 breaths"
    dal: "2026-11-24"
    riconosci: "The child **does not respond** and **is not breathing normally**."
    fare:
      - "Call **112** on speakerphone. If you are alone with no phone, do 1 minute of CPR first."
      - "Give **5 rescue breaths** (in an infant over the mouth and nose), until the chest rises."
      - "Compressions in the centre of the chest, to **one third of its depth** (about 4 cm in an infant, 5 cm in a child), **100–120 a minute**: in an infant with **two thumbs** encircling the chest, in a child with one or two hands."
      - "Then **30 compressions and 2 breaths** (15 and 2 if you have done a paediatric course)."
      - "Use the **AED**: under 25 kg in paediatric mode, if available, with one pad on the front and one on the back."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't stop until help arrives or the child is breathing normally."
      - "If you can't give breaths, don't stand still: at least do the compressions."
  - id: "fratture"
    titolo: "Fractures and sprains"
    motto: "Keep it still, don't straighten it"
    dal: "2026-11-27"
    riconosci: "Severe pain, swelling, bruising, an odd shape of the limb or a part that cannot be moved."
    fare:
      - "Support the part **in the position you find it** (hands, a cushion, an arm sling)."
      - "**Ice wrapped in a cloth**, for about 20 minutes."
      - "Open fracture: cover with a **clean gauze** and press around it if it is bleeding."
      - "Call **112** for open fractures, fractures of the hip, thigh bone, back or neck, very severe pain, or a hand or foot that is cold and numb."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't straighten the limb and don't let them move it."
      - "Don't push the bone back in and don't put ice directly on the skin."
  - id: "freddo"
    titolo: "Hypothermia and frostbite"
    motto: "Into the warm, gently"
    dal: "2026-12-01"
    riconosci: "Intense shivering, cold pale skin, clumsy hands, then confusion and drowsiness. **If they stop shivering, it is serious.** Frostbite: white, numb fingers, ears or nose."
    fare:
      - "Get them to shelter and somewhere warm, **moving them gently**."
      - "Remove wet clothing, dry them and cover them with dry blankets, **including the head**."
      - "If they are awake and can swallow, a **warm, sweet drink**."
      - "If they are confused, drowsy or no longer shivering, call **112**. If they are not breathing normally, start CPR."
    nonfare:
      - "No alcohol: it makes the body lose even more heat."
      - "On frostbite, don't rub and don't warm it with fire or heaters."
  - id: "monossido"
    titolo: "Carbon monoxide"
    motto: "The gas you can't detect"
    dal: "2026-12-04"
    riconosci: "Headache, nausea, dizziness, drowsiness, **often in several people in the same home**, and in pets too. The gas has no smell and no colour."
    fare:
      - "**Open doors and windows and get out** into the fresh air."
      - "Switch off the appliance only if you can do it straight away and without risk."
      - "Call **112**, even if you feel better outside."
      - "Prevention: **annual servicing** of boilers, stoves and flues, and a **carbon monoxide detector**."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't enter a room full of gas if you would be putting yourself at risk too."
      - "Never use braziers or generators indoors, never use a gas oven for heating, never leave an engine running in a closed garage."
  - id: "asma"
    titolo: "Asthma attack"
    motto: "Sitting up and calm"
    dal: "2026-12-08"
    riconosci: "Shortness of breath, **wheezing**, coughing, chest tightness. In severe cases they cannot speak."
    fare:
      - "Have them **sit up, leaning slightly forwards**, with their arms resting on something."
      - "Stay calm and help them breathe slowly."
      - "If they have **their own reliever inhaler**, help them use it as their doctor has explained."
      - "Call **112** if the inhaler isn't enough, they cannot speak, their lips are blue or they are drowsy."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't make them lie down."
      - "Don't underestimate swelling of the lips or tongue: it may be anaphylaxis."
  - id: "batterie"
    titolo: "Button batteries and magnets"
    motto: "Suspicion is enough"
    dal: "2026-12-11"
    riconosci: "A child may have swallowed a button battery or magnets. **Often there are no signs at first**; sometimes coughing, drooling, pain, vomiting or refusing food."
    fare:
      - "Even if you only suspect it: call **112** or go **straight to A&E** (pronto soccorso)."
      - "Bring the **packaging** or an identical battery."
      - "Prevention: toys with a **screw-fastened battery compartment**, new and used batteries kept away from children."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't wait for symptoms: within a few hours the battery can burn the oesophagus."
      - "Don't make the child vomit and don't give food or drink, unless doctors or the Poison Control Centre tell you to."
  - id: "cadute-anziani"
    titolo: "Falls in older people"
    motto: "No rush"
    dal: "2026-12-15"
    riconosci_tit: "What to look for"
    riconosci: "Pain, a knock to the head, confusion, a leg that won't move or a very painful hip."
    fare:
      - "Reassure them and ask where it hurts, whether they hit their head, whether they remember what happened."
      - "Call **112** if they hit their head, take anticoagulants (blood thinners), have severe hip pain, cannot move a leg or are confused."
      - "If they are fine, get up **in stages**: onto one side, onto the knees, then using a sturdy chair."
      - "If they cannot get up: a cushion under the head, a blanket, and wait for help."
      - "Prevention: no slippery rugs, night lights, grab rails in the bathroom, a phone within reach."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't pull them up suddenly and don't force them."
      - "Don't leave them alone in the following hours, especially if they hit their head."
  - id: "botti"
    titolo: "New Year's Eve fireworks"
    motto: "Celebrate without harm"
    dal: "2026-12-18"
    riconosci_tit: "The risk"
    riconosci: "Every year burns, hand injuries and eye injuries, often from illegal or unexploded firecrackers."
    fare:
      - "Only fireworks with the **CE mark**, bought from authorised shops."
      - "Burn: **cool running water for at least 20 minutes**, then cling film or clean gauze."
      - "Bleeding hand: **press hard** and call **112**."
      - "Severed finger: in a **clean, sealed bag**, and the bag in ice."
      - "Injured eye: cover it without pressing and call **112**."
    nonfare:
      - "Don't pick up or relight an unexploded firecracker."
      - "Don't rub the eye, don't remove fragments, don't let the finger touch the ice directly."

    motto: "Ready for anything"
    dal: "2026-12-22"
    riconosci_tit: "What you need"

    fare:
      - "Add a **sheet of useful numbers**: 112, the Poison Control Centre, your doctor."

---

In an emergency the first few minutes matter most, and often the person nearby is not a doctor: it is a relative, a colleague, a passer-by. **Knowing what to do, and what not to do, can change the outcome.**

In this dossier you will find **twenty-three cards**: the ten most common emergencies and thirteen more, from newborns to older people, from the cold to New Year's Eve fireworks. For each one: how to recognise it, what to do step by step, the mistakes to avoid and an **in-depth video with animated drawings** to watch and share (videos in Italian). All the content has been checked against the [2025 CPR guidelines of the Italian Resuscitation Council](https://www.ircouncil.it/linee-guida-rcp-2025/) and of the [European Resuscitation Council](https://www.erc.edu/). The newer cards gain their video as each one is released, up to 22 December.

**In every emergency, the first step is to call [112](tel:112)**: the operator will guide you until help arrives.
