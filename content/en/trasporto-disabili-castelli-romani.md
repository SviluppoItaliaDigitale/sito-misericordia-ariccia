*[‹ English home](/en/)*

A wheelchair, a walking frame, a leg that no longer holds up: when getting around is difficult, every trip becomes an effort for the person and for the family. The Misericordia di Ariccia provides **equipped vehicles and trained volunteers** for **transporting people with disabilities** throughout the Castelli Romani.

![Misericordia di Ariccia volunteers helping a person down the stairs](/img/attivita/attivita-13.jpg)

## The vehicle with a lift

Our equipped vehicle has a **wheelchair lift**: you get on and off **while staying seated in your own wheelchair**, which is safely secured. No tiring transfers, no lifting. For people who walk with some help we also use comfortable **cars**; when medical assistance is needed, the **ambulance**.

**Don't have a wheelchair or crutches?** We will lend them to you for the whole journey: just tell us when you book.

## Which journeys

- **Visits, tests and treatment** in hospital or at specialist centres
- **Day centres**, rehabilitation, therapy programmes
- **School, work and commitments** of everyday life
- **Shopping and errands**, with help from a volunteer
- **Leisure**: a day at the seaside or in the mountains, a party, a ceremony
- Recurring transport, such as [dialysis](/en/trasporto-dializzati/)

## Where

**Ariccia, Albano Laziale, Genzano di Roma, Marino, Castel Gandolfo, Velletri, Lanuvio, Nemi, Rocca di Papa, Lariano** and the surrounding area; for longer transfers, **throughout Italy**.

## Book your transport

Some services can be arranged **through the town council or social services**: ask us how. For everything else, just give us a call a little in advance.

## Frequently asked questions

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I travel in my electric wheelchair?</summary>

Tell us when you book: we check that the **size and weight** of the wheelchair are compatible with the vehicle's lift and restraints, and if not we find an alternative.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can you lend me a wheelchair or crutches for the journey?</summary>

Yes: if you don't have them, **we provide a wheelchair and crutches** during the transport. Let us know when you book, so we can have them ready in the vehicle.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can a family member come with me?</summary>

Yes, as a rule there is room for a **companion**. Let us know when you book.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do you also provide non-medical accompaniment?</summary>

Yes: shopping, errands, a day out, a ceremony. For us social transport is just as important as medical transport, so that nobody is left shut indoors. Find out about our [social services](/en/servizi-sociali/).

</details>
