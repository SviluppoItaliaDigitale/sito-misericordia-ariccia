When people look for a **private ambulance** in the Castelli Romani, this is usually what they need: transport that is **not urgent but comes with assistance**, for someone who cannot travel sitting in a car or who needs someone beside them during the journey. This is exactly the service the Misericordia di Ariccia has provided for over thirty years, with **its own ambulances and trained volunteer rescuers**.

![Misericordia di Ariccia ambulance, Mobile Rescue Unit](/img/attivita/attivita-04.jpg)

## When you need an ambulance

- **Hospital discharges** to home or to a nursing home, on a stretcher
- **Visits, tests and treatment** for people who cannot sit up or who need assistance
- **Transfers between healthcare facilities**, including long-distance ones
- **Returning home** from hospitals in Rome or other cities
- Transport for **dialysis and recurring treatment** (see [dialysis transport](/en/trasporto-dializzati/))

If a stretcher is not needed, a **car** or a **vehicle with a wheelchair lift** is often enough: we will tell you on the phone, so you can choose the most comfortable and economical option. All our vehicles are described on the [Patient & disability transport](/en/trasporto-infermi/) page.

## Who is on board

A crew of **trained rescuers** (level I and II courses, BLSD, i.e. basic life support and defibrillation) and, depending on needs, a **nurse or doctor**. Every ambulance carries a **defibrillator (AED)**.

## Where we go

We start from **Ariccia** and serve the whole of the **Castelli Romani**: Albano Laziale, Genzano di Roma, Marino, Castel Gandolfo, Velletri, Lanuvio, Nemi, Rocca di Papa, Lariano and the surrounding area. For discharges and transfers we reach hospitals in **Rome** and **throughout Italy**.

## How much does it cost?

We are a **volunteer organisation**: we do not charge commercial rates like a private company. We ask for a **contribution** that covers the cost of the vehicle and supports our free services for vulnerable people in the area. Call us with the details of the journey and we will tell you the terms straight away, with no obligation.

## How to book

Contact us **a little in advance** with the date, time, pick-up, destination and whether a stretcher or a companion is needed.

## Frequently asked questions

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do you provide ambulance transport at night or on public holidays?</summary>

We organise transport according to our volunteers' availability: call us to agree a time, including early-morning services or on public holidays. For emergencies, always and only call **112**.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can a family member travel with me in the ambulance?</summary>

Yes, as a rule a **companion** can come along: let us know when you book so we can keep a seat free.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do you provide ambulance transport outside Lazio?</summary>

Yes. We carry out **long-distance transfers throughout Italy**: journeys home, transfers to specialist centres, planned admissions in other regions.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do I need an ambulance or is a car enough?</summary>

If the person can sit up and does not need medical assistance during the journey, a **car** or a **vehicle with a wheelchair lift** is enough. If a stretcher, oxygen or a rescuer's presence is needed, an ambulance is required. If in doubt, describe the situation to us on the phone: we will advise you.

</details>
