*[‹ English home](/en/)*

The Misericordie have almost eight centuries of history and a language all of their own, and the world of emergency care is full of acronyms. Here you will find the words you meet on this website explained, grouped by family. Each entry keeps the Italian term, followed by an explanation in English.

## The words of the Misericordia

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Misericordia (Confraternita di Misericordia)</summary>

A Christian-inspired volunteer association devoted to helping others (literally "Confraternity of Mercy"). The first was founded in **Florence in 1244**: today there are hundreds in Italy, united in the **Confederazione Nazionale delle Misericordie d'Italia** (National Confederation of the Misericordie of Italy). The Misericordia di Ariccia has been active since 1994. The full story is in the [dedicated dossier](/en/dossier/storia-delle-misericordie/).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Confratello e consorella (brother and sister of the confraternity)</summary>

The members of the Confraternita: the women and men who, by enrolling, become part of the Misericordia. No previous skills are needed: we learn everything together. [How to join](/en/diventa-volontario/).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Magistrato (governing board)</summary>

Not a judge! In the Misericordie the Magistrato is **the governing body of the association**, elected by the members' assembly: it runs the Confraternita like a board of directors. Its current members are listed on the [Who we are](/en/chi-siamo/) page.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Governatore (Governor)</summary>

The person who chairs the Magistrato and represents the Misericordia: the equivalent of the association's president.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Correttore spirituale (spiritual director)</summary>

The priest who accompanies the spiritual life of the Confraternita.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>«Che Iddio te ne renda merito» ("May God reward you for it")</summary>

The ancient motto with which the Misericordie greet and thank one another: the volunteer's only "reward" is the knowledge of having done good.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Opere di Misericordia (Works of Mercy)</summary>

The fourteen acts of charity — seven **corporal** (feeding the hungry, visiting the sick…) and seven **spiritual** — that for centuries have inspired the service of the Misericordie. [The dedicated dossier](/en/opere-di-misericordia/).

</details>

## The words of the Third Sector

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>ODV — Organizzazione di Volontariato (volunteer organisation)</summary>

The legal form of our association: a non-profit body that carries out activities of general interest relying mainly on volunteers.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>ETS and RUNTS</summary>

An **ETS** (*Ente del Terzo Settore*) is a Third Sector organisation, the Italian legal category for non-profit bodies; the **RUNTS** (*Registro Unico Nazionale del Terzo Settore*) is the National Single Register of the Third Sector, the public national register in which these organisations are listed. The Misericordia di Ariccia has been in the RUNTS since 28 March 2023 ([transparency](/en/trasparenza/)).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>5x1000</summary>

A share (five per thousand) of IRPEF, Italian personal income tax, that every taxpayer can assign to a non-profit organisation **with a simple signature** on their tax return: it costs nothing extra. Our tax code is **90031910582** ([how to do it](/en/sostienici/)).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>PEC — Posta Elettronica Certificata (certified email)</summary>

An email with legal value, like a registered letter: it is the official channel for communicating with public bodies and institutions. Ours is on the [Contact](/en/contatti/) page.

</details>

## The words of emergency care

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>DAE — Defibrillatore semiAutomatico Esterno (AED, automated external defibrillator)</summary>

The device that can restart a heart in cardiac arrest: you switch it on, attach the pads and **it guides the rescuer with voice prompts**. Anyone can use it, and using it early saves lives.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>BLSD and P-BLSD</summary>

*Basic Life Support and Defibrillation*: cardiopulmonary resuscitation techniques with the use of an AED. The **P** stands for *Paediatric*, the version for infants and children. These are the courses we run regularly ([training](/en/formazione/)).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>RCP — Rianimazione CardioPolmonare (CPR, cardiopulmonary resuscitation)</summary>

The chest compressions (and, where appropriate, rescue breaths) that keep a person in cardiac arrest alive until help arrives. The essential steps are on the [First aid](/en/primo-soccorso/) page.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>ARES 118</summary>

The Regional Health Emergency Agency of Lazio (*Azienda Regionale Emergenza Sanitaria*), which runs emergency medical services (the "118"). Our BLSD trainers are certified by ARES 118. Today, to call for help, you dial **112**.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Trasporto sanitario e trasporto sociale (health transport and social transport)</summary>

**Health transport** is by ambulance or equipped vehicle for medical visits, hospital discharges and transfers; **social transport** accompanies elderly or disabled people in their everyday lives (day centres, therapy). [How to request them](/en/richiedi-trasporto/).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Centro d'Ascolto e Banco Alimentare (listening centre and food bank)</summary>

The **Centro d'Ascolto** welcomes people in difficulty and helps them find their way through practical problems (bills, paperwork, aid); the **Banco Alimentare** distributes food to families in need. [Our social services](/en/servizi-sociali/).

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Servizio Civile Universale (SCU) and the DOL platform</summary>

The Universal Civil Service: a paid year serving the community for young people aged 18 to 28. Applications are submitted on the **DOL** platform (*Domanda On Line*, online application) of the Department for Youth Policies. [All the details](/en/servizio-civile/).

</details>

Can't find a word? [Write to us](/en/contatti/): we will gladly add it.
