*[‹ English home](/en/)*

When getting around becomes difficult, we are here. For over thirty years we have accompanied **sick, elderly and disabled people** with suitable vehicles, trained volunteers and full respect for the person. We operate across the **Castelli Romani** and, for long-distance journeys, throughout Italy.

## How we can help

- **Ambulance** — for those who need assistance during the journey or stretcher transport, with trained crew on board.
- **Car** — for visits, tests and discharges when an ambulance is not required: comfortable, safe and economical.
- **Wheelchair-accessible vehicle** — fitted with a lift: you board comfortably while staying in your own wheelchair.

## Request a transport

The sooner, the better: even **the day before** helps us find the right vehicle and crew. For recurring trips (dialysis, therapy) we agree a schedule together. Contact us with the **date and time**, **pick-up and destination**, **number of people** and any special needs (wheelchair, stretcher, companion).

**Phone / WhatsApp:** [+39 348 4068657](tel:+393484068657) · **Email:** [sede@misericordia-ariccia.it](mailto:sede@misericordia-ariccia.it) — or use the form on the [Request a transport](/en/richiedi-trasporto/) page. Your transport is booked only once we have confirmed it.

## How much does it cost?

We are a volunteer organisation: for transport we ask for a **contribution** that covers the cost of the vehicle and helps fund our free services for vulnerable people. Call us with the details of the journey (date, pick-up, destination, type of vehicle) and we will tell you the terms straight away, with no obligation.
