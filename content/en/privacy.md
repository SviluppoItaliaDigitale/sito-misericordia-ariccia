## Cookies and tracking

This site uses **no** profiling cookies and **no** third-party tracking or analytics. No technical or analytics cookies are installed: you can browse freely, with no consent banner.

## Browsing data

Like any website, the server that hosts it (Aruba) receives the technical data needed to show you the pages, such as your IP address and the page requested, and may record them in its logs for security purposes. We do not use them to profile you.

## Settings saved on your device

The choices you make in the accessibility panel (text size, contrast, “Stop animations”…) are saved **only in your browser** (local storage): they are not cookies and are never sent to us. You can clear them with “Reset all” or by clearing the site data in your browser.

## Requests you send us

The transport request form **does not send anything to our servers**: it prepares a message that you send yourself with your email app or with **WhatsApp**. If you choose WhatsApp, the text of the message (including the details you entered) is passed to the WhatsApp app, run by Meta, under its own terms and privacy policy: if you prefer, use email or phone. When you write to us (email, WhatsApp) or phone us, **we receive the details you give us**: we use them **only to reply** and to organise the service you asked for, without selling or publishing them. Please give only the information needed to organise the service (how the person can travel), **without diagnoses or medical reports**.

## Why we use your data, and on what legal basis

| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone, email, dates and addresses in a request | To reply and organise the transport or service you ask for | Art. 6(1)(b): steps you request and provision of the service |
| Information on how the person can travel (wheelchair, stretcher…) | To send the right vehicle and crew | Art. 9(2)(a): your explicit consent, given by telling us; you can withdraw it at any time |
| Data for receipts and contributions | Tax and accounting obligations | Art. 6(1)(c): legal obligation |
| Technical browsing data (server logs) | Website security | Art. 6(1)(f): legitimate interest in protecting the site |

Giving us your data is optional, but without contact details we cannot reply or organise the service. We do not use your data for advertising and we take no automated decisions about you.

## Who can see it

- The Misericordia's **volunteers and staff** who organise the service, authorised and bound to confidentiality.
- The **technical providers** we use: Aruba S.p.A. (website and email, in Italy). If you choose **WhatsApp**, also **Meta**, under its own terms: in that case data may be transferred outside the European Union, with the safeguards provided (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). If you do not want this, use email or phone.
- **Authorities**, only where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Requests are kept **as long as needed** to handle them and provide the service, then deleted. Accounting records (receipts for contributions) for the period required by law, normally **10 years**. Server logs are handled by Aruba for its own technical security periods.

## Your rights

At any time you can ask to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, **restrict** or **object to** its use, receive it in a readable format (**portability**) and **withdraw** consent already given, by writing to the address below (Articles 15-22 GDPR). We reply within one month. You can also lodge a **complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority** ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/)).
