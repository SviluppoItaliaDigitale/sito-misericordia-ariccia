Sports competitions, village festivals, concerts, dance recitals, processions, public events: for many events a **medical presence is required** (and for all of them it is a good idea). The Misericordia di Ariccia provides cover with **ambulances, trained rescuers and an AED (defibrillator)**, as we have done for years at events across the Castelli Romani.

## What we offer

- **Ambulance with a crew** of qualified rescuers, present for the whole duration of the event
- **Defibrillator (AED)** and first aid equipment
- **First aid post** for events with large attendance
- Help in assessing the event's **medical cover needs** (based on the type of event and number of participants)

## Who it is for

Town councils, parishes, associations and sports clubs, schools, festival committees, private organisers. We operate in **Ariccia and the Castelli Romani** and, depending on availability, in the neighbouring areas.

## Frequently asked questions

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is an ambulance compulsory for my event?</summary>

It depends on the type of event, the number of participants and what the town council and the local health authority (ASL) require to authorise it. Describe the event to us: we will help you **assess the medical cover needed** and decide on the most suitable arrangement.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How much does medical cover for an event cost?</summary>

It depends on the duration, the number of vehicles and rescuers needed and the location. We are a volunteer organisation: we ask for a **contribution** that covers the cost of the service. Fill in the form and we will send you a **quote with no obligation**.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How far in advance should I request the service?</summary>

Ideally **a few weeks before**, especially for summer weekends and patron saint festivals, when there are many requests.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do you also cover events outside the Castelli Romani?</summary>

We operate in **Ariccia and the Castelli Romani** and, depending on our volunteers' availability, in the neighbouring areas. Feel free to ask.

</details>
