*[‹ English home](/en/)*

Good deeds are hard to measure, but they can be told honestly. On this page we gather, **year by year**, the verifiable figures of our service in the area: it is our way of being accountable to those who support us — through the 5x1000, through donations or by giving their time.

## 2025

- **96 people** living in severe deprivation supported with food aid and accompanying measures (Programma Nazionale Inclusione, the National Inclusion Programme, together with the Banco Alimentare del Lazio food bank)
- **2.61 tonnes** of food distributed
- **239 food parcels** funded by the National Inclusion Programme
- **3 young people** from the Universal Civil Service serving alongside vulnerable people (2025-2026 cycle, completed in June 2026)
- **Medical assistance** at events, races and performances in the area, and health and social transport with our vehicles — the stories are in our [news](/news/) (in Italian)

## For more than thirty years

- **Since 1994** alongside the community of Ariccia and the Castelli Romani
- **2 type-A ambulances** and vehicles equipped to transport people with disabilities
- **Deployments in national emergencies**: Versilia (1996), Marche (1997), L'Aquila (2009), Amatrice (2016), the Covid-19 pandemic
- **Young people in the Universal Civil Service** welcomed since 2015: more than **35** across all calls
- **First aid and BLSD courses** open to the public every year

> This page will grow: we are also gathering figures on transport, hours donated and active volunteers, to tell the story of our impact ever more fully. Official documents are in the [Transparency](/en/trasparenza/) section.
